What happens next

Playlists break when the files behind them move. Repairing one means finding where each of its tracks went, so there is something to play again.

  1. 1I look, I don't touchYour folders are scanned, and every track your playlists point at is matched against the files actually found there.
  2. 2You review the matchesAnything uncertain is shown to you with both sides side by side. You accept or reject each one.
  3. 3You confirm the writeA new collection file is written only after you confirm — your playlists keep their names, order and history, and only the broken references inside them change. Your original is left untouched and backed up.
One file is written before you confirm: a tag cache at
%LOCALAPPDATA%\traktor-nml-tool\tagcache.json
It remembers what was read from each file so a second scan is fast. It contains no collection data. Change location · Clear it

Nothing is written to your collection until you confirm at step 4.
