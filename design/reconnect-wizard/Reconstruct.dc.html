What happens next

A playlist can keep its name and lose everything in it. An older collection still holds what was in it, and this puts the contents back without disturbing anything else.

  1. 2The repair is previewedEvery playlist is rebuilt in memory and counted back to you — how many were filled, from which collection, and how many tracks each gained. Nothing is written.
  2. 3You resolve the disagreementsWhere two collections hold the same track with different values, both are shown side by side and you pick which one supplies it.
  3. 4You confirm the writeA new collection file is written only after you confirm. The collection you are repairing is left exactly as it is.
Nothing is written before step 4 — not even a cache. The preview at step 2 reads the collections above and holds the result in memory, so leaving before you confirm leaves every file on disk untouched.

Nothing is written to your collection until you confirm at step 4.
