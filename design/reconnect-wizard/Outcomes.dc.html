Why each is its own status

  • You cannot filter to what you cannot see. Refuted was a sub-count of unmatched: right for the statistics, wrong for the workflow. A withdrawn candidate is not a missing file, and reporting it as one says “gone” about a track the scan found.
  • Refuted starts in the queue, not behind a filter. Nothing that wants a decision is hidden by default.
  • No extra friction to accept. The panel already names the contradicting field and quantifies it; a confirmation on top adds a click the operator learns to dismiss.
  • Neither adds a colour. Yellow says a row wants a decision; the icon and the word say which. The measured palette therefore stands unchanged.

Where a re-encode comes from

A re-encode replaces the file rather than moving it, so every tier keying on the name as written stops matching and the track reads as gone. The two tiers that strip the container sit at loose and filename, levelled on the evidence they carry.

The wizard runs them and surfaces the result as review — without asking the operator to know a --match-confidence flag exists.

Both panels are the same rail as any other row — same keys, same buttons, same undo. Nothing written yet.
